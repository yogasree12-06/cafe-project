A day at the counter

The room changes shape three times a day.

6:30 — 10:00

Morning pour

The ovens go on at five. First loaves and the day's laminated pastries land around seven. Regulars order without speaking.

11:30 — 2:00

Midday hold

Laptops out, the espresso machine runs steady, and the toast board switches to savory. Loudest hour of the day, in a good way.

3:30 — 6:00

Slow wind-down

Filter coffee replaces espresso on the counter. Lower light, longer conversations, last bakes pulled at four.

Where it comes from

We roast what we can drive to.

Beans arrive green from three farms we've visited in person, roasted in twenty-kilo batches in the room behind the counter. What you drink today was roasted Monday. Nothing sits on a shelf long enough to go stale.
